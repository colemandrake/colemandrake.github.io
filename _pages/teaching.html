University of Pittsburgh

HPM 3125: Intermediate Health Economics

PhD · Spring 2020–2022, 2024, 2026

A doctoral course in health economics covering the economics of health care and health insurance markets, including market failures, the behavior of consumers, insurers, and providers, and the empirical literature on health policy.

HPM 3515: Advanced Empirical Microeconomics Methods with Applications for Healthcare Research

PhD · 2025, 2027

A doctoral course in causal inference and applied econometrics, with an emphasis on research designs used to evaluate health policy and applications drawn from health services research.

HPM 2028: Microeconomics Applied to Health

MHA, MPH, and MS · Fall 2019–2023

An introduction to microeconomics, the study of how resources are allocated and the role markets play, applied to health care and public health.

BFAE 2850: Health Economics

Executive MBA · 2024, 2026, 2027

An introduction to the economics of the health care sector for executive MBA students, covering how health care and health insurance markets work and how policy shapes them.
