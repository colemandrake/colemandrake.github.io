## About Me

I am a health economist and associate professor in the Department of Health Policy and Management at the University of Pittsburgh School of Public Health, where I direct the department's doctoral program. I also hold a secondary appointment in the Department of Economics and am an affiliate at the Center for Pharmaceutical Policy and Prescribing and the Hillman Cancer Center. My research has been supported by the National Cancer Institute (NCI), the National Institute on Drug Abuse (NIDA), the Agency for Health Research and Quality (AHRQ), the Commonwealth Fund, the National Institute on Health Care Management (NIHCM), and Arnold Ventures. I received my PhD in Health Services Research, Policy and Administration with a focus in health economics from the University of Minnesota in 2018. I was a health economics fellow at the Becker Friedman Institute at the University of Chicago in 2018. 

My research examines how health insurance markets work and how public policy shapes them, with a focus on the ACA Marketplaces and Medicare Advantage. I have expertise in applied microeconomics, causal inference, and geospatial methods.
